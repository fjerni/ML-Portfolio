GRAPHIC DESIGN · GD 491

The “AI Issue” Zine

A research-driven zine about how AI works, AI sycophancy, and its environmental impact. I designed it to make complex issues approachable and encourage critical thinking.

GD 491 · FINAL PROJECT

The “AI Issue” Zine
The challenge

Help people unfamiliar with AI understand large language models, their limitations, and the wider effects of AI without overwhelming them.

My approach

I shifted from an interactive website to a physical zine, using approachable writing and magazine-inspired design to invite curiosity.

1 / 12
The outcome

A zine concept that explains LLMs and explores AI sycophancy and environmental impact in a format designed to be shared around campus.

Reflection

Balancing accessibility with a direct discussion of difficult issues shaped the project's writing, visual direction, and final format.
