My work

The “AI Issue” Zine

A UX research-driven zine about how AI fundamentally works, AI sycophancy, and its environmental impact. I designed it in order to critically examine complex issues surrounding AI, without alienating users.

My "Nifty TIFFty" Marathon

I am deeply passionate about cross-disciplinary, multi-medium projects. In this way, film can connect to us on a deeper, more empathetic level. This project combines my love of film, culture, critique, and humanity.

Lansing Connect

As Experience Architects, our concern is with the experiences of people. One issue facing internet users today is homelessness. Lansing Connect seeks to compile resources for the homeless population in Lansing, Michigan into one easy to navigate space.
