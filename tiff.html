My “Nifty TIFFty” Marathon

Film brings together so much of what I love: culture, critique, storytelling, and the chance to see through someone else’s eyes.

After attending the Toronto International Film Festival in 2025, I turned my experience into a website that others could follow along with. This project connects my interest in film with my work in digital design.

Project
A personal film festival website
My role
Website creator
Context
Toronto International Film Festival · 2025

01 / Starting point

From attending to sharing.

Attending TIFF gave me a personal experience to build around. I wanted to bring that experience online so someone who wasn’t there could still follow along.

The website became a way to connect two interests: engaging with film and deciding how to present that experience to a reader.

02 / My approach

Make the experience the story.

I chose a website as a place to bring my TIFF experience together. It gave the project a home that I could share with people beyond the festival.

My focus was the connection between the medium and the subject: how could a digital project express my interest in film, culture, and critique?

The threads connecting the project

Film.

Culture.

Critique.

A personal experience, translated into a digital one.

03 / The outcome

A place to follow along.

My “Nifty TIFFty” Marathon brings this experience into a standalone website. The finished project is available to explore online.

Explore the TIFF website ↗

04 / Reflection

Where my interests meet.

I’m drawn to projects that cross disciplines and mediums. This project brings film and web design together around something I experienced firsthand.

What matters to me is the human connection: film can help us understand another perspective, and a website can offer a way to share our own.
